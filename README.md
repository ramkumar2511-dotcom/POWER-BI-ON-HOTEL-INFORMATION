The Hotels Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project designed to analyze, monitor, and understand hotel sales performance through meaningful business insights. It provides a comprehensive overview of sales activities, hotel-wise performance, purchase modes, food category distribution, regional sales trends, and pricing patterns. The dashboard transforms raw business data into easy-to-understand visual reports, helping users make informed, data-driven decisions.

The dashboard highlights important Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), including Total Revenue (31K), Total Quantity (289), and Total Unit Price (11K), providing a quick summary of overall business performance. These metrics help users understand revenue generation, product sales volume, and pricing information at a glance.

The Purchase Mode filter allows users to analyze transactions based on different purchasing channels, such as Blinkit, Instamart, Offline, Swiggy, and Tomato. The hotel-wise quantity chart compares the sales quantity across different hotel brands, including Pizza Hut, Domino's, Burger King, Starbucks, McDonald's, and KFC, making it easier to identify high-performing hotels.

The dashboard also includes a Unit Price by Day visualization to track pricing variations across different weekdays. The Customer Age by Foods and Unit Price by Time donut charts provide insights into food category distribution, customer purchasing patterns, and time-based pricing trends.

Additionally, the Sales by Region and Time treemap presents a detailed breakdown of sales across different geographical regions and time intervals. This visualization helps identify regional sales contributions and understand customer purchasing behavior throughout the day.

The Total Sales and Total Quantity by Region section provides a comparative view of regional performance, while the Total Sales by Foods chart highlights the contribution of individual food categories, including sandwiches, pizza, burgers, noodles, French fries, dosa, and ice cream.

Overall, this dashboard serves as a centralized analytical solution for monitoring hotel sales performance, identifying business trends, comparing product categories, evaluating regional contributions, and discovering opportunities for improvement. Its interactive filters and visualizations make complex sales information accessible, understandable, and useful for business analysis and strategic decision-making.
